BTI TEAM VIDEO — REVIEW. UNDERSTAND. PREPARE.

Professional post-game video review designed around your team’s playing system, tactical principles and coaching philosophy.

Each game is analyzed from a team perspective, focusing on how effectively the players execute the structure and tactical concepts established by the coaching staff.

Key situations are selected and organized into a clear, structured video review that can be used by coaches for team meetings, post-game feedback and preparation for the next game or practice.

The analysis can be tailored to the areas that matter most to your team — from 5-on-5 structure, forecheck and breakouts to zone entries, defensive-zone coverage and special teams.

The goal is to turn game footage into clear, relevant teaching material that supports the coaching staff and helps the team learn from every game.
